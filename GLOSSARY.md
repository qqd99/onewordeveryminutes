# Vocabulary learning

## Language

**Learning language**
The language whose words the learner studies and pronounces.

**Meaning language**
The language used to explain the words being learned.

**Dictionary pair**
A directional dictionary from one learning language to one meaning language.

**Sense**
One published meaning of a word, with its grammatical and usage information.

**Published human-edited entry**
An entry obtained from an attributed dictionary maintained by human contributors. This describes its editorial origin, without guaranteeing that every sense is correct.

**Coverage**
The words in a learning pack for which the selected dictionary pair contains a published meaning.
