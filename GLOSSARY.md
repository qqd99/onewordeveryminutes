# Vocabulary learning

## Language

**Learning language**
The language whose words the learner studies and pronounces.

**Meaning language**
The language used to explain the words being learned.

**Vocabulary row**
A specific published concept or sense with corresponding language cells. Different senses of the same spelling occupy separate rows.

**Language cell**
The published words or phrases expressing a row's meaning in one language, with their source attribution. A definition-only cell can explain a word but cannot supply a learning prompt.

**Language column**
All cells for one language across the shared vocabulary rows. The learning and meaning selections choose columns from the same model.

**Dictionary pair**
A selected learning column and meaning column. Available rows contain a learning form and a meaning cell in those columns.

**Sense**
One published meaning of a word, with its grammatical and usage information.

**Published human-edited entry**
An entry obtained from an attributed dictionary maintained by human contributors. This describes its editorial origin, without guaranteeing that every sense is correct.

**Coverage**
The study entries with both a learning form and a published meaning available in the selected columns.
